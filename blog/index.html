A tentative 50-post plan for the next three months. The sequence is organized as five technical threads: Goldilocks Zone, AI Supercycle, Hybrid Analysis, Software 4.0, and The Holy Grails.

The published Summer 2026 essay remains the prologue. The other titles are working titles that will evolve from Markdown scratch pads into rendered posts.

This is my personal blog. The views expressed here are my own and do not represent the University of Nebraska–Lincoln (UNL) or BMO.
