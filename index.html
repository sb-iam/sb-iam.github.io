Research and Academia

I am a doctoral researcher in the Computer Science Department at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln under the guidance of Dr. Witawas Srisa-an. My work is in hybrid program analysis: combining static analysis, dynamic analysis, compiler infrastructure, and runtime evidence to understand real software systems.

Engineering and Enterprise

I also have industry experience in cloud engineering, ML infrastructure, and AI systems. At BMO, I worked as Principal Cloud Engineer - AI after leading large cloud engineering programs. I am currently on sabbatical from BMO while focusing on my Ph.D. research.

Current platform

VerifyD Platform

VerifyD is my current platform work for hybrid analysis of AI-assisted software development. It focuses on making software changes easier to inspect, test, explain, and review.

The platform direction connects my program-analysis background with practical developer tooling: static checks, dynamic checks, compiler-aware evidence, provenance, and human review.

Key Contributions

  • Hybrid static and dynamic analysis for AI-assisted code changes.
  • Compiler-aware evidence for understanding what changed and why.
  • Runtime validation and trace collection for generated or modified software.
  • Review-oriented summaries that keep human judgment in the loop.
  • Research lineage from Android hybrid analysis and compiler-integrated interpretability.
